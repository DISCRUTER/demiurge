# demiurge
A server made in c from scratch.

The most prominent mythological concept that perfectly describes this is the Demiurge, most famously explored in Gnostic mythology.
In Gnosticism, the creator of the physical universe, often called [Yaldabaoth](https://www.google.com/search?q=yaldabaoth&kgmid=/g/120r4l5s) or the [Demiurge](https://www.google.com/search?q=demiurge&kgmid=/m/02fpl), is a lesser, flawed deity. He creates our material world in imitation of a higher, perfect spiritual realm (the Pleroma). Even though his creation is fundamentally flawed, ignorant, and vastly inferior to the true divine realm that already exists, he continues to build, maintain, and rule over it out of arrogance and blindness.

## Other Mythological and Folklore Parallels
* Greek Mythology (The Curse of Sisyphus & Futilty): While Sisyphus does not create a physical object, his myth is the absolute symbol of doing something again and again despite knowing it is completely pointless. He is condemned to roll a boulder up a hill, only to watch it roll back down, repeating the exact same effort for eternity.
* The Golem of Prague (Jewish Folklore): In many variations of the Golem narrative, characters or sorcerers attempt to replicate God's creation of life. Even though they know a created Golem is a lesser, imperfect, and ultimately destructive imitation of true human life, they are driven by pride or desperation to create it anyway.
* The Myth of Arachne: [Arachne](https://www.google.com/search?q=arachne&kgmid=/m/0bw_3), a mortal weaver, challenges the goddess Athena to a weaving contest. Arachne creates a flawless tapestry, but its subject matter depicts the failures and vices of the gods. She creates a work that is intentionally offensive and spiritually "worse" or more corrupting than what exists, driven by hubris, which ultimately leads to her downfall.
